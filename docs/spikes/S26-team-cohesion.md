## Exact scope of `CanGeneratePawnRelations = false`

`Verse.PawnGenerator.TryGenerateNewPawnInternal` gates its call to `GeneratePawnRelations` at lines 824–827. With this flag false, the vanilla generation pass selecting blood and non-blood relation definitions is skipped. `GeneratePawnRelations` (lines 2047–2092) selects matching-race Pawns from `PawnsFinder.AllMapsWorldAndTemporary_AliveOrDead`; it is not limited to the newly generated group. Initial non-family generation also depends on the kind's `generateInitialNonFamilyRelations`.

The flag additionally makes the vanilla redress/reuse suitability check reject an already-related flesh Pawn (`IsValidCandidateToRedress`, lines 397–400). The existing force-new requirement remains necessary: `ForceGenerateNewPawn = true` independently prevents substituting an already existing world Pawn.

The flag does **not** prevent negative situational opinions, trait friction, randomized compatibility, later organic relationships, interactions, memories, insults or fights. It is not a guarantee about third-party generation hooks. Replace loose prose such as “no rivals” with the precise claim “skip vanilla initial blood/non-blood relation generation”; vanilla hostility is not generally represented by a generated Rival relation.

## `FixedIdeo`: reliable request selection, limited guarantee

`TryGenerateNewPawnInternal`, lines 898–914, chooses `request.FixedIdeo` before faction ideology or a weighted random ideology, when the generated kind does not prevent ideology, the Pawn has an ideology tracker and the Pawn is not a baby. `PawnGenerationRequest.ValidateAndFix`, lines 237–246, corrects contradictory `ForceNoIdeo`/`FixedIdeo` requests and removes fixed ideology for baby/newborn generation. These are reasons to validate eligibility before constructing the request and recheck the returned Pawn.

Use a reference to the organization's existing eligible member ideology, selected deterministically from existing members, for **newly generated** eligible members only. If there is no existing eligible member, the first generated member can establish the ideology for other new candidates in that placement batch. Do not create an organization faction or a new persisted ideology registry. Different existing ideologies remain real established history and cannot be aligned to satisfy the new group policy.

`RimWorld.Pawn_IdeoTracker.SetIdeo`, lines 183–244, is not a harmless post-generation repair: it can record ideology change history, update membership, remove forbidden spouses or bonds, and issue a bond-removal letter. Use `FixedIdeo` in the original request; never call `SetIdeo` on an encountered/bound member to manufacture cohesion. For the supported adult/eligible case, verify `ReferenceEquals(candidate.Ideo, requestedIdeo)` after generation and reject/abort a contradictory candidate rather than rewriting it. Shared ideology is consistency, not agreement with every precept or assurance of no conflict.

## Screening safety and limits

An eligible fully initialized returned candidate can expose its traits, `relations.DirectRelations`, ideology and mutual opinion before binding. These are useful inputs, not permission to edit them. Authoritative post-generation checks remain necessary: `GenerateTraits` applies `PawnKindDef.forcedTraits` and `request.ForcedTraits` before the filtered backstory/random trait passes (lines 1438–1490). Thus request `ProhibitedTraits` alone cannot prove an incompatible forced trait is absent.

Core same-faction opinion screening is meaningful before spawn. It is **not** a complete preview of later social behavior. `Pawn_RelationsTracker.CompatibilityWith` (lines 815–838) combines age difference with a stable pseudo-random Pawn-ID pair offset. `NegativeInteractionUtility.NegativeInteractionChanceFactor` multiplies opinion and compatibility factors separately; Abrasive multiplies the result by **2.3**, while Kind returns zero for that negative-interaction selection path. `InteractionWorker_Insult.RandomSelectionWeight` uses that factor. `Pawn_InteractionsTracker.SocialFightChance` (lines 434–486) then uses the interaction, capacities, hediffs, opinion, trait factors, age difference and genes. Even two Pawns with zero or positive initial opinions can later conflict. Do not reroll Pawn IDs, force Kind, add friendship relations or positive memories to manufacture compatibility.

An opinion query also mutates **transient caches**, although it does not author social history. `SituationalThoughtHandler.AppendSocialThoughts` (lines 90–100) stores the target Pawn as a dictionary key and records the query tick. State refresh is cached for 100 ticks; `SituationalThoughtInterval` prunes expired target caches after 300 ticks or for discarded targets. A retained/suspended existing teammate need not tick immediately, so asking its opinion of a rejected candidate can keep a temporary candidate reference alive until normal invalidation/pruning or load. `ThoughtHandler.ExposeData` saves memories only (lines 30–33): the situational cache is not a new durable roster. A future mutual screen must still test rejected-candidate residue, and should not “clean up” real members' social history to alter opinions.

## Owner-approved 3.2B cohesion policy

The owner accepted **BEST-EFFORT prevention by construction** as the baseline implementation contract:

1. Every first projection uses `ForceGenerateNewPawn = true` and `CanGeneratePawnRelations = false`.
2. Newly generated eligible adults share the selected existing organization member ideology through `FixedIdeo`, with an authoritative returned-Pawn check. Existing members keep their actual ideology and social history.
3. Read returned traits/relations/ideology for generation consistency; contain generation failures with the existing bounded attempts and a diagnostic. Do not broaden retries solely to produce a guaranteed socially harmonious crew.
4. Do not enforce the previously illustrative multi-band opinion floors or a broad mod-dependent trait blacklist in 3.2B. Numeric floors and high-band trait prohibitions are not source-proven guarantees. Any future stronger construction policy, such as a narrowly defined Abrasive prohibition, requires a separate explicit design decision; it is not part of the accepted 3.2B scope and must never remove an existing trait.
5. Keep pairwise opinion collection in the disposable cohesion runtime probe. Record before-spawn and after-spawn opinions in both directions, active opinion thought causes, faction, ideology, compatibility, rejection counts, generation time and log errors. This distinguishes meaningful initial reads from claims that every active mod's workers are safe or every future crew will remain friendly.
6. Once bound, never change relations, opinions, thoughts, memories, traits or ideology to maintain team cohesion. Later insults, fights, conversions and deaths are physical history.

This resolves the technical uncertainty conservatively without blocking ordinary group generation. Owner acceptance is recorded; 3.2B does not promise that an established professional crew cannot arrive with clear dislike. The source cannot establish that stronger promise for every map-dependent or modded thought. Any future stronger pre-bind screen requires a separate design decision defining its exact numeric threshold and eligible bands, followed by the disposable runtime probe before calling that policy validated.

## Frequency and validation still required

**Generated-crew hostility frequency: NOT MEASURED.** No full RimWorld game runtime or normally initialized active-mod Def pipeline is present in this environment. A bounded headless experiment over handcrafted traits/Pawn shells would measure the chosen fixture distribution, not the frequency of valid vanilla/modded generated crews; it would be misleading to report that as a crew frequency estimate. No such estimate is asserted.

The future disposable runtime probe should use a fixed recorded seed set, maximum eight eligible members, the actual role kinds and active mod list, and a bounded sample count (for example 32 crews per supported construction policy, explicitly a sample choice). Run an unscreened construction baseline and the elected policy, record all candidate attempts, and report the fraction of crews with at least one ordered pair meeting the predeclared hostility threshold. Recheck after spawn and a normal thought refresh; do not force relations, memories, traits or artificial opinion changes. A result on one mod list is not a guarantee for others. Verify existing teammates' history and the retained registry before/after rejected candidates, and verify cleanup/save/load is clean.

S26 therefore remains **PARTIAL (source audit)** until that runtime evidence is attached. BEST-EFFORT cohesion is owner-approved without claiming a runtime PASS; stronger enforcement is outside 3.2B and requires a separate future design decision.
